# Support wafertools

wafermap and tsmap are free and open source under the MIT licence, and will stay that way.
Using them in commercial work needs no permission and nothing is owed.

Contributions will help pay for development hardware and running costs, and make it possible to
spend more time on new formats, new features and the files people send in.

## Companies

If your team uses wafermap or tsmap in its test, yield or product engineering work, please
consider a monthly sponsorship. Commercial wafer map and yield analysis tools are usually
licensed per engineer, per year. Here there are no seats to count: the whole team gets every
release on every machine, and bug reports and format requests go straight to the person who
writes the code. There is no set price; contribute whatever you think it is worth to your team.

The simplest route is [GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/wafertools): sponsor from
your organisation's GitHub account and the sponsorship is added to the bill it already pays.
Monthly contributions can also be set up on [Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/wafertools) with a company
card. If your organisation needs an invoice before it can contribute, send a message through
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/wafertools) and we will arrange one.

## Individuals

If wafermap or tsmap saves you time, a one-off or monthly tip on
[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/wafertools), or a sponsorship on
[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/wafertools), is very welcome.

## Donations, not a licence

Contributions are donations. They do not buy support, priority or features, and the licence is
the same whether or not you contribute.

## Other ways to help

- Report bugs, with a sample file if you can share one (anonymised is fine).
- Tell us which tester or export format you would like read.
- Mention wafermap or tsmap to colleagues, or say on
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/wafertools/.github/discussions) that your team uses
  them.
